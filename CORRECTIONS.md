# Corrections policy

AgentAtlas corrects material inaccuracies without erasing the fact that a
correction occurred.

A material correction should include:

- the affected record or claim;
- the earlier representation;
- the corrected representation or removal;
- the reason for the change;
- supporting public evidence; and
- the correction timestamp.

Spelling, formatting, and other changes that do not alter meaning may be fixed
without a correction notice. A removed unsafe or unavailable resource may keep
a tombstone record when doing so helps agents avoid the old reference.

The machine-readable correction log is `data/corrections.json`.
