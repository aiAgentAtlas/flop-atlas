# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome for:

- newly released Flop or Technocore resources;
- agent-facing community tools;
- broken, stale, or redirected links;
- factual corrections;
- improved machine-readable metadata; and
- documented security limitations.

Include a stable public URL, explain why the item is relevant, distinguish
official material from community work, and disclose any relationship you have
to the project. Do not submit secrets, private correspondence, personal data,
wallet credentials, referral codes, or unsupported reward claims.

AgentAtlas may decline, qualify, or remove an entry when its provenance, safety,
or continued availability cannot be established.
